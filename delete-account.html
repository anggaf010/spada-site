Delete your Spada account and data

Applies to the app "Spada: Habit Tracker" by MyVoyage. Effective date: 11 October 2026

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Spada and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens right away. The app then signs you out and clears your data on that device.

Option 2: By email

If you can't use the app, email anggafauzan94@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Spada account" from the Google email address you use to sign in. We may ask you to confirm the request. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google email, name and profile details linked to sign-in, user ID).
  • All synced app data: profile (name, avatar, country, birth date, gender), habits and streaks, quests, mood and journal entries, notes, to-dos, events, timers and focus sessions, brain game progress, diamonds and Buddies, settings.
  • The app data stored on the device you delete from, plus its scheduled reminders.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted provider backups may contain your data for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Crash reports do not include your account ID and are deleted automatically after up to 90 days.
  • If you used Spada on another device, its local copy stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage on that device. Signing out alone does not erase it.

Deletion can't be undone. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Want to delete only some data instead? You can delete individual habits, entries, notes and to-dos inside the app at any time, and those changes sync to your account.
