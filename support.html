FAQ

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with Google so your data can be backed up and synced. Your data is also saved on your phone.

Is my data synced between devices?

Yes. When you are signed in with the same Google account, your habits, moods, journal, notes and other data sync to your account. If you edit on two devices at the same time, the app asks which copy to keep.

Does Spada work offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your phone and sync the next time you are online.

My reminders don't show up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Spada in your phone settings. On Android 12 and later, also allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time. Battery saver modes can delay notifications.

How do I connect my calendar?

Open Notes and connect a calendar. Spada asks for calendar access and only reads events to show them; it never changes your calendar.

What are diamonds and Buddies?

You earn diamonds by completing habits. Spend them to unlock Buddies for your collection. They are just for fun and cannot be bought.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, French, Spanish and Japanese. Change the language in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. More details on the account deletion page.
